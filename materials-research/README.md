# Sustainable Material Selection for FFF in Custom Assistive Technology

This folder contains a graduate-level portfolio revision of a materials review originally developed during my **Humanos 3D internship in Medellín, Colombia**.

The report asks a practical engineering question:

> **How should a designer select a lower-impact FFF material when environmental performance, mechanical function, user safety, repairability, and realistic end-of-life options must all be considered together?**

## Report Scope

The review compares **PLA, recycled PET-based feedstocks / rPETG, and PHA** and expands the comparison beyond basic material properties. It examines life-cycle boundaries, process energy, print-time emissions, manufacturing-structure-property coupling, service life, repairability, and the importance of matching sustainability claims to real disposal or recycling infrastructure.

The report also revisits a food-waste-derived composite-filament concept as a **research hypothesis and validation pathway**, rather than treating projected benefits as experimentally demonstrated results.

## Graduate-Level Additions

The portfolio revision adds a more rigorous engineering structure, including:

- evidence appraisal and source hierarchy;
- manufacturing-structure-property analysis for FFF parts;
- risk-based material qualification for assistive devices;
- service-normalized life-cycle reasoning;
- constraint-based multi-criteria material selection;
- uncertainty and sensitivity analysis;
- ASTM-oriented mechanical and environmental test planning;
- a research-grade validation roadmap for new composite feedstocks; and
- an expanded scholarly and standards-based reference set.

## Important Scope Boundary

This review is an engineering synthesis and design-screening framework. It does **not** certify any polymer, filament, or printed component for clinical, medical, food-contact, prolonged skin-contact, or other safety-critical use. Final material selection depends on the exact filament grade, additives, process parameters, geometry, loading, cleaning conditions, and applicable standards or regulations.
